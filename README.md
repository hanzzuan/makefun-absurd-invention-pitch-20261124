## The pitch

> Meet the Grudge Mug: a coffee mug that remembers every time you left it unwashed and refuses to hold hot drinks until you apologise to it properly.

A ridiculous invention for a tiny everyday annoyance: the mug you keep leaving unwashed in the sink, now holding a grudge and going on strike until you say sorry.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, no network, no randomness) writes exactly one sentence — 147 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 -P -s main.py
```

Running it twice produces byte-identical output.
